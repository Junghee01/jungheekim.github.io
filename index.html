About

Hello! I am Junghee Kim.

I am interested in developing data-driven and learning-based approaches for resilient transportation systems, particularly in data-scarce environments or extreme disasters.

I hold a master's degree in Civil and Environmental Engineering from KAIST and have over six years of experience in the Korea Road Traffic Authority.

My work experience focused on redesigning hazardous intersections through accident data analysis, geometric improvements, and traffic signal adjustments, along with traffic accident analysis using simulation and field investigations.

After that, I applied machine learning techniques to mobility data. During my master’s degree, I identified behavioral changes in subway usage during COVID-19 using DTW-based clustering. I also developed predictive models for transit demand under large-scale events and varying weather conditions while working at a transportation-related company.

Research

COVID-19 Mobility Behavior Change Detection
Identified distinct mobility behavior patterns during COVID-19 using DTW-based hierarchical clustering on subway ridership data (232 stations in Seoul). The study revealed heterogeneous recovery trends and enabled region-specific policy recommendations.
[Paper]

Deep Learning-based Mobility Prediction
CNN-based traffic prediction model applied to Seoul road networks.
[GitHub]

Project

Transit Demand Prediction under Events and Weather Conditions
Developed a two-stage XGBoost-based framework to predict metro congestion during large-scale events, integrating weather and demand data; achieved up to 98% accuracy and identified tipping points in ridership sensitivity.
