Kazakhstan Field Trip

This page describes my experience in Kazakhstan.

In July 2023, I traveled to Kazakhstan with my church community to learn about Central Asian culture, religion, and local community life. I interacted with local communities and reflected on how different beliefs shape people's lives.

I conducted a wallet-making workshop for local students in Kazakhstan and also participated in community clean-up activities, including weed removal. Through these experiences, I had meaningful interactions with local residents.
