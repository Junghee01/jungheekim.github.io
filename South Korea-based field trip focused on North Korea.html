South Korea-based field trip focused on North Korea

This page describes my experiences in South Korea related to the division of the Korean Peninsula.

I participated in a field trip to the border area near North Korea, where I was able to look out over the Imjin River and reflect on the reality of the division between North and South Korea.

We also visited a church where we worshipped together with North Korean refugees, shared rice cakes, and joined in the service as a community.
