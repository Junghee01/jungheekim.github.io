South Korea-based field trip focused on North Korea

This page introduces my experiences in South Korea related to the division of the Korean Peninsula.

In August 2024, I participated in a field trip to the border area near North Korea, looking out over the Imjin River and reflecting
on the reality of the division between North and South Korea.

We also visited a church where we worshipped together with North Korean refugees, shared rice cakes, and joined in the service
as a community.
