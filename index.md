# Junghee Kim

Prospective PhD applicant in Machine Learning, with interests in transportation systems and data-scarce environments

## Research Interests

- Machine Learning for Transportation Systems
- Learning under Data Scarcity (e.g., Graph-based and Physics-informed approaches)
- Transportation Network Optimization and Mobility Behavior Modeling

## About
I hold a master's degree in Civil and Environmental Engineering from Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology(KAIST) and have over six years of experience in the public transportation sector.
My experience includes traffic signal operations and data-driven analysis, such as identifying high-risk intersections using accident data, redesigning road infrastructure, and analyzing traffic accidents through simulation and field investigations to identify contributing factors.
Building on this domain knowledge, I have recently worked on applying machine learning to mobility data, including clustering-based analysis of behavioral changes during COVID-19 and predictive modeling of transit demand under large-scale events and weather conditions.

I am interested in developing data-driven and learning-based approaches for transportation systems, particularly in data-scarce environments and network optimization.
